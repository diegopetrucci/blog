> Erin has used a phrase in her blog that always resonates with me in these situations. It goes something like, “It’s a piece of furniture that belonged to your grandmother, it’s not your grandmother.” Try reminding yourself that it’s an inanimate object. Learning to enjoy your memories, without needing the object associated with that memory, I think, is a huge step in this process and it takes time
