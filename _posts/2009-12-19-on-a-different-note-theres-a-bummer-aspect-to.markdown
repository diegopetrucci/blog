> On a different note: there’s a bummer aspect to friend-recommended content as well, however: if you stay within the circle of your Internet friends and their interests, you miss out on exposure to completely novel ideas. Luckily (?) the Internet carpet-bombs you with so many random, unexpected, never-before-seen things on a daily basis, there’s probably little actual danger right now of cutting yourself off completely from things you didn’t know you needed to know.
