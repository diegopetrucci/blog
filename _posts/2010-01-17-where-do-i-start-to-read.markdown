I like [Organizing your way](http://www.organizingyourway.net), it’s a nice site with helpful tips. But take a look at this screenshot:

![](/blog/assets/imported/77f1d76e1f3f-tumblr_kwelk3oUom1qa6n2i.png)

Even with Chrome (one of the most light browser) and a resolution of 1280×800, I can barely read the first two lines of the real content.

I don’t say they should remove everything from their site except the post, but a different placement of the various elements would improve the site’s readability.  
For example, who needs to see the full tree of categories at the top of the post? It could be placed at the bottom instead. With this little change 3-4 more lines could be displayed and a casual visitors could instantly get the sense of the post without having to scroll down.

Note that this is note an attack to the Organizing your way designers, it’s just some thoughts about design and minimalism.
