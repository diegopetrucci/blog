> But I think that’s part of my revulsion at the direction our on- and off-line lives are moving. People seem to think that texting their friends counts for interacting with them. Many times we count as ‘friends’ people we’ve never even met. In my book, a person is an acquaintance until you shake their hand, then they become a friend. If I’ve been carrying on an online conversation with you, don’t take that personally, I really do like you all…but you’re still acquaintances until I see you face to face and treat you to a cup of coffee.  
> Call me old fashioned, but I like person to person interaction better than LOLing.
