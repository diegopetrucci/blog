> The plasters were removed using both fast and slow methods, with a randomisation process used to decide which was used first on each student. Subjects were asked to rate the pain on a scale of 0 to 10, with 10 being the “worst pain imaginable”.  
> Fast removal achieved an average pain score of 0.92, while slow removal was significantly more painful at 1.58.  
> The average overall pain scores for women were also significantly lower than for men.
