> For sentimental items and nick-knacks take a digital photograph and donate or sell the physical item.  
> A lot of time with sentimental items it’s not the physical item that has the meaning it’s the memories and feelings it invokes when you see it. You’ll get all of those same feelings when you look at the picture.
