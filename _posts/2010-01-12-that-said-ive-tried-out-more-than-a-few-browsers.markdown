> That said, I’ve tried out more than a few browsers over the years and almost always wind up “switching back” after a day or two (or at most: a week or two). With that in mind, I was more than expecting to go “Meh, back to Safari…” by the end of the week.
>
> But at the end of that week, I’m beginning to think that Chrome might stick around for quite some time as my day-to-day browsing browser.

[Daring Fireball Linked List: A Week With Chrome for Mac](http://daringfireball.net/linked/2010/01/12/chrome-friesel)

Same experience here. I used to use Firefox on Ubuntu and Windows, when I switched to Mac I tried Safari, Firefox, Camino, Opera and Chrome.

I have spent some time using Safari, but it just didn’t look right. I felt something was missing in my browsing experience.  
Then I tried the beta of Chrome and I’ve never used any other browser.

I know, until some days ago I couldn’t install extensions, but I didn’t care because except for the ads that adblock on firefox blocked, Chrome was better.

Moreover, Chrome is a minimalistic browser. It’s extremely fast and light and has no unnecessary buttons.

The only one feature I’d want is something like the DownloadStatusBar extension for firefox that makes the download bottom bar smaller and disappears when a download is complete.
