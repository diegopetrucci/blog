> \[Visiting his grandpa\] But the whole time, I had one big question on my mind. But when I saw him drifting asleep, I knew it was my last chance. So I did: “What’s it like to know you’re going to die soon?”  
> His eyes lit up! His face lit up! He said, “It’s wonderful! It’s the most natural thing of all. Every baby born is going to die. Everything else in my life has been great, so I’m sure this will be, too. It’s exciting!”  
> I quietly got up to go. As I got to the door, he heard me open it, and seeing me still staring at him, he said, “Don’t worry about me. I’m happy.” I said, “Oh I don’t need to worry about you.” He died the next day.

[Derek Sivers](http://sivers.org/papabill)
