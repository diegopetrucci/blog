**5 Things Challenge, Week Four**

*Introduction:*

> The day I read of the [10 Things Challenge](http://simpleorganizedlife.com/10-things-challenge-week-one/), I made the decision to partecipate to it. It consists in picking 10 items per week that you don’t longer use or plan not to use it in the future, so you can free up some space in your house.  
> I’ll only pick 5 items per week because I live with my parents and they do not share my ideas about uncluttering and minimalism so I can only unclutter my bedroom.

This week I got rid of:

1.  A Photoshop CS2 manual. I can find all the tutorials I need by searching on Google, so this book is a waste of space. Toss it.
2.  A postcard from my uncle. Will I ever use it? No. Do I feel something special for it? No. Toss it.
3.  A black ball. I painted it some years ago. I took a photo of it, and toss it.
4.  A gym tool. I have another one that’s better, so it’s a useless duplicate. Toss it.
5.  The lyrics of a Green Day album. It belongs to a friend of mine so I returned it to him.

Maybe this week has been the easiest one. I wish I’d have stuff like this week’s to get rid of, it’s so easy and liberating.
