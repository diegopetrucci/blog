> “We are always getting ready to live, but never living.”
>
> Most of us are doing this every single day. We plan, we plot, and we say “I will do that when I ….”. But are we doing all that ‘getting ready’ and missing out on the bigger picture – this is life, and this is it. You get one shot, one go ’round, one chance. And if you are always busy thinking about tomorrow, you are truly missing out on today.
