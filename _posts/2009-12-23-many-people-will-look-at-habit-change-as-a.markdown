> Many people will look at habit change as a short-term thing. Like, I just gotta stick with this diet for a month (or eight weeks, or whatever), and then I made it. Wrong. Habit changes should be for life. Don’t do short-term fads, quick changes that you want to stick with for a little while and then you’re done.
