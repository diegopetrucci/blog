Today I noticed I have 200+ feeds in Google Reader. It wouldn’t be a problem if those blogs wrote new posts every once in a while, but many of them are updated daily. So I spend nearly 2/3 of my web-time reading them and making sure I do not miss anything important.

I just said *important*, but what does it really mean to me? There are severals topics I like to be updated on, like the iphone, web trends, minimalism, lifehacker-kind of stuff, and some minor ones. When something important happens I see the *big news* repeated in many of the blogs I follow. So what?

I realized I can keep myself updated with a few feeds without – most important – reading tons of posts everyday. So I decided to delete some subscriptions on a weekly basis. I didn’t set a number, but at least I must delete 3 feeds. It would be great if I deleted about 10 feeds per week, but I don’t care at numbers.

Obviously, I let myself adding new feeds if I find something interesting. If they are not valuable they will be quickly deleted.

Let’s see how it goes.
